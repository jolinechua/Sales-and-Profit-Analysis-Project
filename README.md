# **Sales and Profit Analysis Project**
This project leverages Tableau to analyze sales and profit trends across key markets and product categories. 
By examining data spanning four years (2019–2022), this analysis identifies key drivers of performance and provides actionable recommendations to enhance profitability.

---

## **Overview**
The analysis focuses on:
- Identifying top-performing markets and their contribution to overall profitability.
- Investigating sub-categories with negative profit margins.
- Proposing strategies to optimize costs and improve sales, such as adjusting shipping fees and implementing bundling techniques.

---

## **Insights**
1. **Market Analysis**:
   - The APAC, EU, US and LATAM markets contribute significantly to overall sales and profits.
   - Technology and Office Supplies categories drive the majority of profits.
2. **Profitability Challenges**:
   - The Furniture category, particularly Tables, incurs consistent losses due to high shipping costs and discounts.
   - Despite high sales volumes, Tables exhibit negative profit margins.
3. **Recommendations**:
   - **Adjust Shipping Costs**: Implement fixed or proportional shipping fees to reduce company overhead.
   - **Optimize Discounts**: Reassess discount policies to balance customer incentives and profitability.
   - **Bundle Products**: Use market basket analysis to identify complementary products for bundling.

---

## **Visualizations**
The Tableau workbook includes:
- **Market Performance Dashboard**: Visualizes sales and profit contributions by region.
- **Sub-Category Trends**: Highlights profitability trends for specific product categories.
- **Forecasts**: Projects sales and profit trends for key sub-categories.
- **Market Basket Analysis**: Identifies complementary products to improve sales strategies.
